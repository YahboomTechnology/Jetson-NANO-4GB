Solid State Drive (SSD) Screw Removal Precautions: The Jetson Orin Nano official kit comes with anti-loosening adhesive on the SSD screws. When removing the SSD for the first time, be sure to use a screwdriver to vertically tighten the screw, loosening it by the first turn. Do not repeatedly apply force slowly before loosening, as this may strip the screw and make subsequent removal impossible.
